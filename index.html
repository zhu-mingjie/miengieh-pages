Turn web pages into PDF files with a simple Chrome extension. Save useful articles, reference pages, and reading material to revisit later.

01 / MADE TO KEEP

From your browser
to your files

Save web pages as PDF

Export the page you are viewing in Chrome and keep a PDF copy on your computer.

Meaningful filenames, automatically

Use the web page title as the default filename, so your saved PDFs are easier to recognize and organize.

Read it again, anytime

Open your saved PDF when you need it—even when you are offline. Keep useful information close at hand for work, study, or personal reading.

Keep your references together

Organize exported PDFs in your own folders, alongside your notes and other documents.

02 / THREE SIMPLE STEPS

How it works

  1. 01

    Open the web page you want to save in Chrome.

  2. 02

    Open Save Web as PDF and start the export.

  3. 03

    Save the PDF to your computer for later reading.
